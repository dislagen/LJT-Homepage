Hello! I am **Junteng Liu (刘俊腾)**. I am a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on **natural language processing** and **machine learning. My research interests include:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
